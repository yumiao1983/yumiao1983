What is TravelNDC?+

TravelNDC is an AI-native workspace that helps airlines and travel sellers shop, sell, service and optimise modern airline retailing.

Is TravelNDC built for airlines, agencies or both?+

Both. TravelNDC connects airline retail teams, travel agencies, OTAs, OBTs and distribution partners through workflows designed for their needs.

Which airline content can we access?+

TravelNDC brings together rich airline content, branded fares and ancillary products through modern distribution connections.

Can TravelNDC connect to our back-office systems?+

Yes. The platform is designed to fit beside your existing travel technology stack and operational workflows.

How does AI support offer creation and servicing?+

AI helps teams surface relevant offers, interpret servicing options and turn distribution data into actionable insight.

How quickly can our team get started?+

Our travel experts work with your team to plan the right onboarding path for your content, markets and workflows.
